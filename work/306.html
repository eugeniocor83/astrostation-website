01 — Concept

A single-storey house in Los Angeles, transformed rather than replaced. The footprint and the long, low roofline stay; the pale siding and white brick give way to black vertical timber, a dark roof and deep-set openings. Inside, the house is being rebuilt room by room in warm timber, veined stone and woven wall finishes.
